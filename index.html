I'm Kailin Xu, and I work to build better aging through research, tech, and care.

About

I am an economist, biologist, and epidemiologist by training, and currently am Research Associate II at Marcus Institute for Aging Research at Hebrew Seniorlife, Harvard Medical School.

I explored a lot on what I wanted to do and I found building in field of aging is where my heart lives, and I want to use data, technology, and design to build better care for older adults.

Currently, I'm working to build NLP model to detech BPSD (behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia) uisng nursing home EHR data, predictive ML model for rehabilitation needs, lead clinical trials on sensor-based frailty assessments, and analyze Medicare data to inform care planning decisions for older adults. Previously, I've interned at Charles River Associates,Basys.ai, Marathon Venture Partners, and Everly Health.
