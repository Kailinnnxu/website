# Scene Images

Place your pixel-art PNG scene images in this folder.

## File Naming

Name your scene files according to your schedule.json configuration. For example:
- `desk-1.png`
- `desk-2.png`
- `stretch-1.png`
- `afternoon-1.png`
- `afternoon-2.png`
- `evening-1.png`
- `default.png` (used when no time block matches)

## Image Requirements

- Format: PNG format
- Pixel-art style (will be displayed with pixelated rendering)
- Recommended: Square or 4:3 aspect ratio for best display
- The images will be displayed full-screen with `object-fit: contain` to maintain aspect ratio without cropping

## Current Placeholder

Until you upload your images, the app will try to load these files and show a broken image icon. Once you add your PNG files, they will automatically be used.
